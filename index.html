    Field guide

    Start the run

    Start with 500 points, a 1911, bare fists, two frags, and two flashbangs. Hits and kills earn points. Rounds get tougher, and brutes arrive every fifth round.

    Gear and ammunition

    Buy weapons at chalk outlines or pay 950 points to roll the Mystery Box. Wall weapons refill for half price. Ammo crates refill the gun you’re holding and cost more for upgraded weapons.

    Perks and upgrades

    Perk machines and wall weapons vary by map. Use Pack-a-Punch to upgrade your weapon for 5,000, then 10,000, then 20,000 points. Draw your melee weapon first to upgrade it.

    Map interactions

    Hold the interact button to revive teammates, or rebuild the window boards in The 1 Room Challenge. Turn on the power in Westminster. Unlock the Nitrogen Fists at Hollow Estate for a dash attack.

    Power-ups

    Collect Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points, Nuke, Fire Sale, and Full Restore before they vanish. Moving keeps you clear of zombies that throw projectiles when they cannot reach you.

    Zombie camos

    Earn zombie kills with each weapon to unlock these. The last three are animated. Every camo, from this list or from Multiplayer, works in both modes.

    In VR the menus appear on a panel in front of you: point with the right controller, pull the trigger to press (grip changes a drop-down backwards, right stick scrolls). Health, ammo and score are on your left wrist.

    Touch screens: drag on the left to look, tap or hold on the right to shoot, use the stick for movement (push it to the edge to sprint) and the buttons for jump, reload, grenade, melee, swap and killstreak.

    No aim button: shots go where your crosshair points, and aim assist helps when an enemy is near it.
