    Weapon mastery and camos

    Kills in Multiplayer level your weapons and unlock these camos. Zombie camos can be equipped here too.

    Zombies

    Survive endless rounds of the undead, solo or with up to 3 friends in a co-op lobby. You start with a pistol and 500 points. Zombies get stronger every round and a pack of brutes arrives every fifth round.

    10 points per hit50 per body kill100 per headshot kill130 per katana kill (the katana needs more swings every round)F buy / interact
    • Doors (1000 to 2000) open new wings of the estate
    • Mystery Box (950): a random weapon. The RayGun is rare and one-shots everything
    • Pack-a-Punch: 5000 to upgrade your weapon, 10000 to upgrade it again
    • Soda machines: red health, green fast reload, yellow double damage and fire rate, blue self-revive (the machine runs dry after 4 buys), purple run speed, orange third weapon

    Zombie camos

    Earn zombie kills with each weapon to unlock these. The last three are animated. Every camo, from this list or from Multiplayer, works in both modes.

    Right controller aims and holds the weaponRight trigger fireLeft trigger lethalLeft grip tacticalBoth grips field upgradeRight grip reload, hold: interactA jumpB swap, hold: armor plateX crouch / slide, hold: prone / diveY pause menuLeft stick moveLeft stick click sprint (twice: tactical)Right stick click katanaRight stick left/right snap turn, up ping, down fire mode

    In VR the menus appear on a panel in front of you: point with the right controller, pull the trigger to press (grip changes a drop-down backwards, right stick scrolls). Health, ammo and score are on your left wrist.

    Touch screens: drag on the left to look, tap or hold on the right to shoot, use the stick for movement (push it to the edge to sprint) and the buttons for jump, reload, grenade, melee, swap and killstreak.

    No aim button: shots go where your crosshair points, and aim assist helps when an enemy is near it.
