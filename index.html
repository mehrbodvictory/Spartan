    Zombies

    Survive endless rounds of the undead in the Hollow Estate, an abandoned mansion with a front lawn, a back garden and a crypt. Play solo or with up to 3 friends in a co-op lobby. You always start the same way: a 1911 with 500 points, your bare fists, 2 frags and 2 flashbangs. Your Multiplayer loadout does not carry over. Zombies get stronger every round and a pack of brutes arrives every fifth round.

    10 points per hit50 per body kill100 per headshot kill130 per melee killF buy / interactV tap: quick melee strike, hold: draw your melee weapon
    • Doors (750 to 2500) open new rooms of the mansion
    • Wall weapons: buy guns straight off the wall (600 to 4500). Buy the same gun again for half price to refill its ammo
    • Ammo crates: refill the weapon you are holding for 500, or 2000 once Pack-a-Punched, or 4000 after the second upgrade. Ammo is never restored for free between rounds
    • Mystery Box (950): a random weapon. The RayGun and the Nitrogen Fists are rare: both one-shot almost everything. The Nitrogen Fists replace your melee slot, and while they are drawn (hold V) the aim button is a rocket DASH that smashes through zombies (short cooldown)
    • Pack-a-Punch (in the crypt): 5000 to upgrade your weapon, 10000 to upgrade it again. Draw your melee first (hold V) to upgrade your fists instead. Upgraded weapons come out with a camo that exists only in this map
    • Power-ups drop from zombies: Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points, Nuke, Fire Sale and Full Restore. Run into them to pick them up before they fade
    • Soda machines: red health, green fast reload, yellow double damage and fire rate, blue self-revive (the machine runs dry after 4 buys), purple run speed and shooting while sprinting, orange third weapon
    • Do not camp on furniture: zombies that cannot reach you will hurl their guts at you instead

    Zombie camos

    Earn zombie kills with each weapon to unlock these. The last three are animated. Every camo, from this list or from Multiplayer, works in both modes.

    In VR the menus appear on a panel in front of you: point with the right controller, pull the trigger to press (grip changes a drop-down backwards, right stick scrolls). Health, ammo and score are on your left wrist.

    Touch screens: drag on the left to look, tap or hold on the right to shoot, use the stick for movement (push it to the edge to sprint) and the buttons for jump, reload, grenade, melee, swap and killstreak.

    No aim button: shots go where your crosshair points, and aim assist helps when an enemy is near it.
